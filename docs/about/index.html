About the Mission

We are students who love space and games.

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Our challenge was to help kids train like real astronauts. So we built a game where you can't just press a button and win: you plan, you repair, you wait for messages from Earth and you make your own choices.

Local event · Phoenix, Arizona

See our project on spaceappschallenge.org ↗

What kids practice

  • Planning energy, air and food
  • Fixing problems step by step
  • Reading real space data
  • Thinking alone when Earth is far away

Built with

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Unity Version Control
  • Astro
  • Canva
  • NASA open data
