The Science Behind the Game

Everything in Signal Lost is inspired on real robots and real NASA data.

How long does a message take?

Earth → Moon

1.3 seconds

The Moon is close, so a message arrives almost right away. Sam can help you almost live!

Earth → Mars

3 to 22 minutes

A message from Earth can take up to 22 minutes to arrive, so astronauts have to make smart choices on their own. That is why Sam's tips arrive late in the game!

Space weather is real

NASA DONKI · space weather database

Solar storms

In May 2024 the Sun sent out a burst of huge storms, the strongest to hit Earth in about 20 years. The storms in the game come from those real records. When the alarm sounds, shield your base!

MSL RAD · radiation detector on Curiosity

Radiation on Mars

The Curiosity rover carries a detector called RAD that measures radiation on the surface of Mars. We used its readings from the same storms to set the danger in the game.
